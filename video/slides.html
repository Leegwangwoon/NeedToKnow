How it works

The agent plans. The wallet proves.

01 · MINIMIZE🤖 Claude agentReads any request and reduces it to the narrowest yes/no predicates.
02 · CONSENT✅ You decideConsent card per proof, or a standing policy for low-risk ones.
03 · PROVE🔐 On-device walletZK proof over a bank/issuer-signed commitment. ~45 ms.
04 · VERIFY🏢 Other agentChecks issuer signature + proof. Learns 1 bit. Records nothing raw.
🧱 Privacy wall: the LLM only sees a schema of what can be proven, never your values. A hijacked agent has nothing to leak.
Every disclosure is appended to a hash-chained consent ledger.
